# Sample customer recordings

These four recordings are synthetic speech generated locally with macOS `say`, converted to 16 kHz PCM WAV with `afconvert`. They contain fictional conversations for the company workspace playground, not customer recordings or statements about Dust’s commercial offering.

The transcripts and customer context live in `src/components/pod-workspace/fixtures.ts`; the Atlas incoming-call transcript lives in `src/components/pod-workspace/engine.ts`. The recording player labels these samples as synthetic.
